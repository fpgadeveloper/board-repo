## Serial
- CAN x1

## Extras
- Drive control FMC card for 2 BLDC, 3 DC, or 1-2 stepper motors
- 6 MOSFET half-bridges, 9A each (42V x 18A = 756W total power capability)
- Supports PWM frequencies of 200kHz and higher
- Current sense amplifiers with two-channel simultaneously-sampling A/D converter
- Encoder / Hall sensor interfaces
- 30V sensor inputs and actuator outputs
- Brake chopper to dissipate brake energy
- Onboard temperature sensors
- EMC filters on drive outputs
- Designed for Enclustra Universal Drive IP core

## Notes

The FMC-DR2 is not compatible with Enclustra base boards combined with Xilinx Zynq UltraScale+ modules. Functional testing is limited to the Drive Evaluation Kit.
