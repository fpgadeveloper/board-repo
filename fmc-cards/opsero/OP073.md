## Notes

### Stacking and pass-through

The card is stackable: it uses only the gigabit transceiver lanes and the two GBTCLK reference clocks. Every other FMC signal (LA/HA/HB, CLK0/1, I2C, JTAG, GA, PRSNT) passes straight through to a stacked mezzanine, so no LA pin controls the card itself. VADJ is not used by the card (passed through), and there is no PEDET signal or slot power control at the FMC connector.

### SSD reset (I2C I/O expander)

The M.2 slot resets are driven by a TI TCA9536 I/O expander at 7-bit address 0x41 on the FMC I2C bus (SCL C30 / SDA C31, shared with the FRU EEPROM).

| Bit | Mask | Signal | Description |
|-----|------|--------|-------------|
| P0  | 0x01 | PERST_A# | Slot 1 reset, active low, pulled up |
| P1  | 0x02 | PERST_B# | Slot 2 reset, active low, pulled up |
| P2  | 0x04 | PRSNT_M2C_L | Present signal of a stacked mezzanine |
| P3  | 0x08 | — | Not connected |

Registers: 0x00 input, 0x01 output, 0x02 polarity, 0x03 configuration (1 = input). All pins are inputs at power-up, so the SSDs leave reset without any I2C access.

### FRU EEPROM

M24C02 at 0x50, moved to 0x54 when a stacked mezzanine is present.
