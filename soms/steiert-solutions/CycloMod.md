## Flash
- QSPI 16MB

## Extras
- Raspberry Pi RP2350 MCU (dual Arm Cortex-M33 + dual Hazard3 RISC-V 150MHz 520KB SRAM)
- SparkFun MicroMod (M.2 E-key edge) form factor
- 12MHz reference crystal shared between FPGA and RP2350
- 22 x 22 mm module (<1 sq in)
- Open-source hardware (KiCad design on GitHub)
- FPGA configured by RP2350 over JTAG (Altera JBC player) or from external SPI flash; openFPGAloader via PICO-DirtyJTAG
