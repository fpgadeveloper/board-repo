## Notes

VADJ (Zynq banks 10-12 + FMC LA/HA) and VIO_B (bank 13 + FMC HB) are generated by two LP873220 PMICs, default 2.5 V (LVDS-capable). The user manual states both rails can be changed by software reprogramming the PMICs — there is no System Controller and the board does not read the FMC IPMI EEPROM; other voltages are the user's responsibility. The 1.2-3.3 V range is inferred from the HR-bank VCCO options (1.2/1.35/1.5/1.8/2.5/3.3 V), not enumerated by the manual.
