## Notes

Each FMC+ slot has its own VADJ rail (FMC1_VADJ–FMC4_VADJ, 10 A each) generated by a dedicated IS66066 regulator; the user guide states the FMC bank I/O standard is 1.8 V on all four slots and documents no jumper or programming mechanism, so VADJ is effectively fixed at 1.8 V.
