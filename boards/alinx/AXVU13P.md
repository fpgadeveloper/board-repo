## Expansion
- FMC+ "FMCP0" VADJ 1.8V
- FMC+ "FMCP1" VADJ 1.8V
- FMC+ "FMCP2" VADJ 1.8V
- GPIO Header x1

## User I/O
- LEDs x8

## Extras
- SMA differential I/O (2x, FPGA HPIO)

## Notes

Each FMC+ slot has its own VADJ rail (FMC1_VADJ–FMC3_VADJ, 10 A each) generated by a dedicated IS66066 regulator; the user guide states the FMC bank I/O standard is 1.8 V on all three slots and documents no jumper or programming mechanism, so VADJ is effectively fixed at 1.8 V.
