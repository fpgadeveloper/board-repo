## Video
- MIPI CSI x5
- MIPI DSI x3

## Expansion
- Raspberry Pi x1

## Extras
- 5 camera/display FPC ports: 2x 4-lane hard D-PHY CSI (up to 2.5Gbps/lane) and 3x 4-lane soft D-PHY CSI/DSI (up to 1.5Gbps/lane); the 3 soft ports are configurable as CSI or DSI
- Camera/display FPC connectors follow the Raspberry Pi Zero 22-pin CSI pinout
- Video I/O also supports OpenLDI, LVDS, SLVS and parallel standards in addition to CSI/DSI
- Built-in clock generator
- Onboard configuration Flash with dual-bitstream fail-safe boot
- Onboard MCU for user-specific configuration
- Single 5V supply via the 40-pin HAT header
- 65 x 56 mm Raspberry Pi Model B compatible HAT form factor
