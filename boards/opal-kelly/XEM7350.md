## Notes

FMC Vadj (LA and HA pin groups, HR banks 12/15/16) is generated by an onboard switching regulator adjustable from 1.2 V to 3.3 V, controlled through the FrontPanel Device Settings; the board reads the FMC IPMI EEPROM at boot and can set Vadj accordingly. The HB group is powered by the mezzanine-supplied VIO_B_M2C rail (1.2-1.8 V, HP bank 32) rather than Vadj, and is unconnected on the -K70T variant.
