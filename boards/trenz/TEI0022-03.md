## Notes

FMC VADJ is generated by an Enpirion EN5335QI whose VS pins select 1.2 V /
1.25 V / 1.5 V / 1.8 V / 2.5 V / 3.3 V (0.8 V also exists but is not usable
with the Cyclone V). Selection is made before power-on with DIP switch S8
(S8-1..3), routed through the board-management MAX 10 firmware to the
regulator's VID pins — the FMC IPMI EEPROM is not read. The TRM does not
document a factory-default switch position.
