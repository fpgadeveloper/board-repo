## Flash
- QSPI 4MB

## USB UART/JTAG
- Type-B JTAG/UART

## Expansion
- Pmod x6

## User I/O
- LEDs x8

## Extras
- 100MHz MEMS oscillator (SiT8008, 1.8ps RMS cycle-to-cycle jitter)
- Ultra-low-noise 3.3V LDO (0.6uV RMS) powering the Pmod ports
- 4 jumper switches usable as configurable inputs (SPI/I2C capable, with pull-ups)
- External JTAG header
- Power from USB and/or external 4.5-6V DC jack with reverse-polarity protection
- Series 200 ohm protection resistor on every Pmod I/O pin
