## Notes

FMC VADJ is jumper-selectable via header J3: 3.3 V with no jumper fitted, 2.5 V (pins 1-2), 1.8 V (3-4), 1.5 V (5-6) or 1.2 V (7-8). Set the jumper before power-on — the board does not read the FMC IPMI EEPROM.
