## Notes

VADJ on the FMC LPC connector (J7) is fixed at 1.8 V, generated by a resistor-set buck regulator (Enpirion EN6347QI on power-solution-1 boards, MPM3650 on power-solution-2 boards). There is no jumper or software mechanism to change it, and the board does not read the FMC IPMI EEPROM — the user guide's "adjustable FMC power regulator" refers to the regulator hardware, not a user-settable rail.
