## Notes

FMC V-ADJ is generated by a dedicated, dynamically adjustable DC/DC converter; factory default is 1.8 V with 2.5 V and 3.3 V options. The voltage is changed by serial commands on the power manager (BMC) console and can be stored in the power manager's non-volatile memory - the board does not read the FMC IPMI EEPROM automatically.
