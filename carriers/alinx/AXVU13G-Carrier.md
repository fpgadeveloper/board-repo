## Notes

VADJ is fixed at 1.8 V on both FMC sites. Each site has its own rail — FMC1_VADJ (3 A) for the FMC+ (J23) and FMC2_VADJ (3 A) for the FMC HPC (J24) — both generated by a dual-output regulator (U38, EZ8345). The user guide documents no jumper or programmable selection mechanism, and the board has no system controller, so FMC modules requiring other VADJ voltages are not supported without board rework.
