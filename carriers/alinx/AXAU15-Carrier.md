## Notes

VADJ for the FMC HPC connector is generated by a dedicated ETA1471 regulator (U175) and defaults to 1.8 V; a jumper cap selects 1.2 V instead (discrete 1.2 V / 1.8 V — the board does not read the FMC IPMI EEPROM). Note the FMC LA signals connect to FPGA banks 64/65 on the SoM, which are powered at 1.8 V by default.
