## Expansion
- FMC LPC "FMC1"

## Extras
- 2x 40-pin Anios I/O extension pin headers
- System controller
- 5 to 12V DC single supply (USB bus power with restrictions)
- Hirose FX10 connectors for Mercury / Mercury+ modules
- Temperature range 0..+70 C
- Micro USB 2.0 device interface also exposes SPI and I2C

## Notes

The Mercury+ PE1-200 (ME-PE1-200-C) is end of life (EOL); after stock depletion it will no longer be available. Enclustra recommends upgrading to the PE1-300 or PE1-400 variant.

The available features depend on the equipped Mercury FPGA/SoC module and the selected PE1 board model. The -200 variant omits the low-jitter clock generator, system monitor, power control and current sense fitted on the -300/-400 variants.
