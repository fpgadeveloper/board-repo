## Notes

FMC VADJ supports 1.2 V / 1.5 V / 1.8 V (discrete values), generated by
regulator U8 whose VID pins are driven by the System Controller CPLD
(MachXO2, U17); DIP switch S5D is the documented user input for FMC_VADJ
voltage selection, interpreted by the CPLD firmware. The exact firmware
mapping and boot default are documented on the Trenz wiki (TEBF0818 CPLD
Firmware page), and no automatic FMC IPMI EEPROM negotiation is documented.
The FMC_VADJ rail also supplies the mated SoM's adjustable I/O banks via the
B2B connectors.
